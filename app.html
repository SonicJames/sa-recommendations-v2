About the job

West Alamo Dental Care, supported by Heartland Dental, is looking for a General Dentist to join our team in San Antonio, TX. You'll step into a full schedule from day one in a modern, digital practice with an experienced support team behind you.

You'll provide comprehensive care for patients of all ages, plan treatment with the autonomy to practise the way you were trained, and mentor a team that takes pride in its work.
